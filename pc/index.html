Gyropad for Windows

The companion app your PC needs, so the phone in your hands can drive the game on your screen.

Important note

Windows will probably show a blue “Windows protected your PC” box the first time you run the installer.

That is SmartScreen, and it appears for every new program that has not yet been downloaded by enough people. It is not a virus warning, and nothing is wrong with the file.

Click More info, then Run anyway.

The one thing worth checking is that you got the file from this page. The button below downloads straight from the project's own GitHub releases — never from a mirror or an advert.

Setting it up

  1. Run the installer on your PC No administrator password needed. It installs just for you.
  2. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi Or plug the phone in and turn on USB tethering — Gyropad works over the cable too.
  3. Open Gyropad on your phone and tap Connect Your PC appears in the list by name. Tap it once and you're driving.
