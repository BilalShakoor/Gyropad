Privacy Policy for Gyropad

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gyropad ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

Who we are

Gyropad is developed by Sharper Studio. You can contact us at support.sharper@gmail.com.

What Gyropad does

Gyropad turns your Android device into a steering wheel and gamepad for racing games on a Windows PC. It reads your device's motion sensors (gyroscope and accelerometer) and your on-screen touch input, and sends them to the Gyropad PC companion program running on your own computer, over a local Wi-Fi network or a USB cable that you set up yourself.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data.

Gyropad has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of our own. The app never sends any information to us or to any third party.

Specifically:

  • Motion and touch input is processed on your device and sent only to the PC you have paired with, across your own local network or USB cable. It is used to control your game and nothing else. It is not recorded and it does not leave your local network.
  • Your device name (for example "Pixel 7") is broadcast on your local network while you are pairing, so you can recognise your phone in the PC program's device list. This stays on your local network.
  • Your settings - control layout, sensitivity, and similar preferences - are stored only on your device.
  • Crash information. If the app crashes it writes a technical log to a private folder on your device to help with diagnosis. This file is not transmitted anywhere. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

We do not ask for, and cannot access, your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or files.

Network access

Gyropad requests internet and Wi-Fi permissions solely to communicate with the Gyropad PC program on your local network. The app makes no connections to the wider internet.

Children's privacy

Gyropad is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data security

Gyropad holds no personal data on any server, because it has no servers. Data sent between your phone and your PC travels only across your own local network or USB cable.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time - for example if a future version of the app adds advertising or paid features. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support.sharper@gmail.com.
